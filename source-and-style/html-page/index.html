The History of Jazz

Jazz is an amalgamation of various musical styles across the 20th century. It originated in New Orleans and consisted of blues, ragtime, and spirituals.

Five jazz musicians performing, each with different instruments.

The evolution of jazz can be divided into several distinct periods, each characterized by unique musical styles and cultural contexts.

  • 1920s: The Jazz Age
  • 1930s: Swing Era
  • 1940s: Bebop
  • 1950s: Cool Jazz
  • 1960s: Free Jazz
  • 1970s: Fusion
  • 1980s: Neo-Bop
  • 1990s: Acid Jazz
